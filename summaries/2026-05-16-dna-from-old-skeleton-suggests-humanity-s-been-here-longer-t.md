# DNA from old skeleton suggests humanity’s been here longer than we thought

**URL:** https://arstechnica.com/science/2017/10/dna-from-old-skeleton-suggests-humanitys-been-here-longer-than-we-thought/
**Category:** history
**Date:** 2026-05-16
**Type:** Short Summary

---

• A genome from a Stone Age skeleton in southern Africa (predating the Bantu expansion) clusters with modern Khoe-San, but reveals that present-day Khoe-San have genetic input from an East African population that had already interbred with Eurasians, undermining earlier assumptions of their isolation.
• Comparisons between the ancient genome and modern populations push the Khoe-San divergence to 285,000–365,000 years ago, with the most extreme split (Mandinka) at roughly 356,000 years — far older than the previous genetic estimates of 100,000–150,000 years.
• The results imply that anatomically modern humans may have existed for at least 300,000 years, placing our origin about halfway between today and the Neanderthal–Denisovan split, and matching the timing of Middle Stone Age technology.
• These age estimates are highly sensitive to the chosen mutation rate and generation time, and may be inflated if the Khoe-San lineage absorbed archaic human DNA, a possibility that is difficult to rule out.
