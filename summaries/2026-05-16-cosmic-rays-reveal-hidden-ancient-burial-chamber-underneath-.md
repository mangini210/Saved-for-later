# Cosmic rays reveal hidden ancient burial chamber underneath Naples

**URL:** https://arstechnica.com/?p=1939194
**Category:** history
**Date:** 2026-05-16
**Type:** Short Summary

---

• Researchers used muography, a non-invasive imaging technique that detects cosmic-ray particles, to discover a hidden burial chamber 10 meters (33 feet) beneath a densely populated district in Naples, Italy.
• The newly identified structure is a rectangular chamber measuring approximately 6.5 by 11.5 feet (2 by 3.5 meters) and likely dates back to the late fourth or early third century BCE.
• To capture the data, scientists placed nuclear emulsion detectors in an underground cellar for 28 days in 2018, allowing them to map voids where dense rock failed to block the flow of muons.
• The chamber is believed to be the tomb of a wealthy individual or family from the ancient Hellenistic city of Neapolis, similar in design to other nearby aristocratic "Ipogeo" tombs.
• This breakthrough demonstrates the efficacy of muon tomography for urban archaeology, as it allows for the discovery of ruins in areas where traditional excavation or seismic testing is impossible.
